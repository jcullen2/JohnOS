# Lesson: The Brief engine pattern

The legacy Brush Park Brief is the **proven pattern to port** for every
monitoring producer — do not rebuild from scratch.

- **Config-driven Python**, ~40 sources.
- **Haiku triage** — cheap model does the first-pass filter; expensive reasoning
  only on what survives.
- **HTML render** at the end.

Port this shape for inbound, sourcing, political, sports. Keep the retire-only-
after-replacement rule (spec §9).

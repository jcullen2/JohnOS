# Ruling: the difference between a static update and a living system

**2026-07-09, JC.** The test for every feature: *does tomorrow's Daily differ
because of what I did in today's?* If not, it's a report, not an organism.

Three corrections that made it living:
1. **Voice isn't a style note, it's a corpus.** Drafts must be written against
   `/context/voice.md` (built from real sent mail), and JC's edits must feed back
   into it. Generic "professional" drafts are a failure even when polished.
2. **Sourcing is the product, not a feed.** It gets its own first-class surface
   with per-candidate feedback (More/Pass/Get-me-in/Watch) that updates
   `state/sourcing-prefs.json` and changes the next scan. Burying it in a KNOW
   digest was wrong.
3. **Every review action must persist and be consumed** — rulings.jsonl,
   sourcing-prefs, voice-corrections, thesis counters. The retro is the integrator;
   inline loops change behavior immediately. See `/context/feedback-loops.md`.

Corollary (still true): KNOW is where scope creep attacks; the retro's recurring
job is cutting it back.
